BIRYANI

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Oil
  • Meat or protein
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Onion
  • Tomatoes
  • Biryani masala powder
  • Turmeric powder
  • Yogurt (curd)

Recipe

  1. Marinate the Chicken: Mix the chicken with yogurt, half of the ginger-garlic paste, half of the biryani masala, red chili powder, turmeric, and salt. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the Onions: Heat oil in a large pot and fry the sliced onions until golden brown. Remove half of the onions to use later for layering.
  3. Cook the Chicken Gravy: In the same pot with the remaining onions, add the rest of the ginger-garlic paste and whole spices. Add the marinated chicken and tomatoes. Cook on medium heat until the chicken is tender and the oil separates from the masala.
  4. Boil the Rice: In a separate large pot, boil plenty of water with salt and whole spices. Add the soaked basmati rice and cook until it is 70% to 80% cooked (firm to the bite). Drain the water.
  5. Layer the Biryani: Layer the partially cooked rice over the chicken gravy. Top with reserved fried onions, chopped mint, coriander, and the saffron milk.
  6. Steam (Dum): Cover the pot tightly with a lid or foil. Cook on very low heat for 10 to 15 minutes. Let it rest for 10 minutes before gently mixing and serving.

PASTA

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Oil
  • Cheddar Cheese:
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Onion
  • Milk
  • Red chilli
  • Crunch
  • Salt and Black Pepper

Recipe

  1. Boil Macaroni: Boil 1 cup macaroni in 3 cups salted water for 6 to 8 minutes until soft yet firm (al dente). Drain and save half a cup of the starchy water.
  2. Make the Masala: Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a pan. Add 1/2 teaspoon chopped garlic and 1/3 cup chopped onions; cook until soft.
  3. Add Tomatoes and Spices: Add 1 cup chopped tomatoes, 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder, 1/2 teaspoon coriander powder, and salt. Cook until the mix is thick and pulpy.
  4. Mix and Serve: Stir in chopped veggies or 2 tablespoons tomato ketchup if you like. Add the cooked macaroni and a splash of reserved water, toss well, and garnish with fresh coriander.

PIZZA

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Pizza sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Capsicum
  • Onion
  • Olives
  • Chicken
  • Oregano
  • Chili flakes

Recipe

  1. Spread pizza sauce on the dough.
  2. Add mozzarella cheese.
  3. Top with vegetables and chicken.
  4. Sprinkle oregano and chili flakes.
  5. Bake at 220°C for 15–20 minutes.

BURGER

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Chicken patty
  • Cheese slice
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Mayonnaise
  • Ketchup

Recipe

  1. Cook the chicken patty until golden.
  2. Toast the burger buns.
  3. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup.
  4. Add lettuce, tomato, onion, cheese and patty.
  5. Close the bun and serve hot.

CHICKEN KARAHI

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Tomatoes
  • Oil
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Green chilies
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Coriander leaves

Recipe

  1. Heat oil and fry garlic and ginger.
  2. Add chicken and cook until light brown.
  3. Add tomatoes and spices.
  4. Cook until oil separates.
  5. Garnish with coriander and green chilies.
